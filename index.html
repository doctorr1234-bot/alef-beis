How is doing

Which letters to practice

The games only ask about the letters switched on (green) below. Tap any letter to switch it on or off, or use the buttons to switch a whole row of the Alef Beis at once. "Meet the Letters" always shows everything.

Letter style

Choose how the letters are drawn, so he learns to recognize them in different print. "Mix" changes the style with every question.

Baseball and Football: names or sounds

"Names" asks for a letter by its name ("Hit the Beis"). "Sounds" asks for the sound it makes ("Hit the letter that says b"). "Mix" switches between them. Alef and Ayin are silent, so they are only asked by name.

Baseball

With four balls, three wrong taps is a strikeout, and three outs end the game. With three balls there are no strikeouts.

Replace a letter with a new recording

The game already speaks with the recording built into it. To redo a letter, tap it here, say its name, and tap again to stop (it stops by itself after 3 seconds). That new recording is kept on this device only and is used instead of the built-in one. "Delete all recordings" goes back to the built-in voice.

Stars and the store

Every right answer earns a star. spends stars in his store to dress up his character, so the star count goes down when he buys something. You can hand out bonus stars as a reward, or start over.
